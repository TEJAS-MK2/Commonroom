# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Commonroom aims to be a welcoming place for people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. Everyone participating in the project or its community is expected to help make it respectful, constructive, and safe.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Be considerate, respectful, and patient.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to feedback.
- Share constructive criticism and accept it gracefully.
- Respect differing opinions and experience levels.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Help keep discussions accessible and on topic.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Hate speech, sexual harassment, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without consent.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated bad-faith behavior.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or sustained hostile discussion.

## Scope

This policy applies to project spaces and interactions related to Commonroom, including issues, pull requests, code reviews, and conversations in the public chat.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, preserve relevant context and report it privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation in project spaces, close discussions, or take other proportionate action. They will consider the context and impact of the behavior. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
